On this page, you’ll find general advice on how to use and manage your Vibranium and Arch Linux setup. Before continuing, make sure to read [Project Philosophy](Project%20Philosophy.md).

## Update your OS regularly

As mentioned in the Philosophy page, you are still using Arch Linux, which means you are responsible for maintaining your system. It is strongly recommended to update Arch regularly to avoid unexpected issues later on.

If your system has gone a long time without updates, make sure to check the news on the [main page](https://archlinux.org/) before upgrading. You may need to perform manual intervention or migration steps depending on the changes introduced.

## You still need the Hyprland wiki

While Vibranium makes many things easy to configure through *Vibranium Settings* (`CTRL ALT V`), you will still need the Hyprland wiki if you are not familiar with its configuration syntax and, now, Lua as well.

Yes, there are already many well-documented live examples in `~/.config/hypr/hyprland.conf.d/*.lua`, but sometimes that alone is not enough, especially when configuring advanced [Keyboard Shortcuts](Keyboard%20Shortcuts.md).
