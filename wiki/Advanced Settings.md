Vibranium comes with two types of settings: `settings` and `settings.advanced`. Both are located in `~/.config/vibranium`, but only the latter is intended to be edited manually.

The `settings` file stores options configured through Vibranium Settings (`CTRL ALT V`), while `settings.advanced` is used to customize the behavior of internal scripts that should not be modified directly, as doing so would break future updates.

Take a look at `settings.advanced` for more information and feel free to experiment with it.
