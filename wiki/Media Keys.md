Vibranium includes a set of handy utilities that are automatically used when you press media keys on your keyboard, such as Play/Pause or Next/Prev.

When pausing the currently playing track, the audio doesn’t stop abruptly. Instead, the volume gradually fades out to silence and fades back in when resumed. The fade time is configurable. The same behavior applies when switching tracks. This works with both regular players and MPD.

You can also enable a "Now Playing" notification, which appears when you unlock a previously locked session. Optionally, it can display album art.

All related settings are available in *Vibranium Menu* (`CTRL ALT V`) -> *Settings* -> *Media Control*.
